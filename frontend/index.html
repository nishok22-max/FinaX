How a Rescue Executes
✓Flash-loan the debt asset from Aave — no capital required upfront.
✓Repay the borrower's debt, improving HF immediately.
✓Pull & swap just enough collateral (via aToken allowance only — never a private key) to cover the flash loan + fee.
✓Return leftovers to the borrower, then a HealthGuard check: if HF, debt, leverage, or cost aren't strictly better, the entire transaction reverts.
Unaudited hackathon PoC — Arbitrum One mainnet-fork tested (13/13 Foundry tests). Non-custodial: the borrower's key never touches the backend.
